### Which English accent is the oldest? 哪一种英语口音历史最悠久？

### Episode 260817 / 17 Aug 2026 第260817集 / 2026年8月17日

据估计，英国拥有大约 40 到 60 种地方英语口音。许多人认为，听上去更优雅、更具格调的南方口音才是英国上流社会人士或历史剧中人物所说的英语。不过，只要对英国的语言发展历史稍加了解，就会发现南方口音很可能并非英国最古老的口音。英语的演变历程比大多数人想象的更加复杂和曲折。阅读本文，了解英语发展史上的几个重要阶段，以及最古老的英语口音最有可能来自哪个地区。

**词汇：language 语言**

**词汇：language 语言**

**1** Would you like to speak with a British accent? What if I told you that there is no singular 'British' accent? In fact, linguists estimate that there are 40-60 regional accents across the UK, some of which exist very close together geographically. For instance, you can listen to Manchester's ' **Mancunian** ' accent and then Liverpool's distinct ' **Scouse** ' accent after just a 50-minute car or train ride. Some southern English accents, such as ' **Received Pronunciation** ', are associated with **social prestige**, but can the south claim to have the oldest English accent? Or does another UK region take the **title**? The story is messier than you might expect.

**1** 你想用英国口音说话吗？如果我告诉你，没有一种统一的“英国口音”呢？事实上，语言学家估计英国有40到60种地区口音，其中一些地理上非常接近。例如，只需50分钟的车程或火车旅程，你就能听到曼彻斯特的“ **曼彻斯特** ”口音，然后听到利物浦独特的“ **斯考斯** ”口音。一些南方英语口音，如“ **标准发音** ”，与 **社会声望** 相关，但南方能否声称拥有最古老的英语口音？还是说其他英国地区会接手 **这个称号** ？故事比你想象的要混乱。

  
  
**2** The first version of English, Old English, **originated** in the 5th and 6th centuries when **tribes** from northern Germany arrived in east England, a region now called 'East Anglia'. Their Germanic languages mixed with existing Latin and Celtic languages and became Old English. "It makes sense to think of East Anglia as being the **source** of the earliest variety of English as we know it," says Kerri-Ann Butcher, a lecturer in English Language at the University of Leeds. Over the next few centuries, English **absorbed** many other languages arriving from places like the Netherlands and Scandinavia. The associated **linguistic** changes did not happen in an orderly way across the country.

**2** 英语的第一个版本——古英语， **起源** 于5至6世纪，当时来自北德的 **部落** 抵达英格兰东部，即现称“东盎格利亚”的地区。他们的日耳曼语与现有的拉丁语和凯尔特语混合，形成了古英语。利兹大学英语讲师凯瑞-安·布彻说：“把东盎格利亚视为我们所知最早英语变体的 **源** 头是合理的。”在接下来的几个世纪里，英语 **吸收** 了许多来自荷兰和斯堪的纳维亚等地的语言。相关的 **语言** 变化并非全国范围内有序发生。

**3** With all these different influences, the English language became messy and inconsistent. But in 1476, some spelling norms became **standardised** with the arrival of the **printing press**. Nowadays, the words 'blood', 'food' and 'good', which all contain two letter Os, sound different, but back then, they might have **rhymed**. Between the 12th and 18th centuries, something known as the **Great Vowel Shift** happened, where some vowel sounds across the country changed. For example, the vowel sound in 'foot' and 'strut' used to be pronounced the same, but then people in the south began pronouncing 'strut' differently. Danielle Turton, sociolinguist at Lancaster University, says the northern pronunciation is absolutely the original one. "It was the south that **branched off** and created this new vowel."

**3** 在这些不同影响下，英语变得混乱且不一致。但到了1476年，随着 **印刷术** 的出现，一些拼写规范开始 **标准** 化。如今，“blood”、“food”和“good”这些词都包含两个字母O，听起来不同，但当时它们可能会 **押韵** 。12至18世纪间，发生了所谓 **的大元音转变** ，全国范围内的一些元音音素发生了变化。例如，“foot”和“strut”的元音音以前发音相同，但南方人开始用不同的发音发音。兰卡斯特大学社会语言学家丹妮尔·特顿表示，北方的发音绝对是最原始的。“是南方分支 **出来，创造** 了这个新元音。”

  
   
**4** So, **there you have it**. While East Anglia may have been the home of the earliest English sounds, it did not stick to the original pronunciations like accents in northern England did. And while many think Received Pronunciation is an older-sounding English, in fact, it didn't exist until the 18th century. The north of England wins!

**4** 所以， **这就是全部内容** 。虽然东安格利亚可能是最早英语音的发源地，但它并不像英格兰北部的口音那样坚持原始发音。虽然许多人认为标准发音（Received Pronunciation）是一种发音较古老的英语，但实际上它直到18世纪才出现。英格兰北部获胜！
